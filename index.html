Graduation

Skin scan, from a photo to a doctor.

Classification of Skin Cancer Using Machine Learning. BSc Computer Science, El-Obour, 2022–2023.

Team leadExcellent84% accuracy83% precision82% recall

I led CS-07. A patient photographs a spot. The Android app, written in Java, sends it to a Django REST API. A Keras network compares it with labeled benign and malignant lesions and returns a class. A malignant result suggests a doctor near their location, with the fee and the hours. If they cannot travel, they book a video consult and pay with Vodafone Cash, InstaPay, or another method. The same app keeps accounts, lesion history, chat, articles, and nutrition notes.

The model

Images are scaled to 0–1, then augmented with flips, zoom, contrast, and rotation. Convolutions and max-pooling feed a dense layer of 128 units and a sigmoid output. About 2.1 million parameters, trained 100 epochs with Adam and binary cross-entropy. Held-out test: 84% accuracy, 83% precision, 82% recall. 2,637 training images, 660 test images, two classes.

The path

Scan, nearby doctors, then a consult if travel is not possible. The doctor card shows the specialty, the city, the hours, the rating, and the fee. Booking picks a day and a time. The video call runs in the app, and payment is Vodafone Cash, InstaPay, or another method.
